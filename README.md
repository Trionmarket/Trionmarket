# Trion

**The market for compute.**

- **CMPT** is the benchmark price of one H100 SXM GPU-hour in US dollars — a trimmed, weighted composite of six named marketplaces and clouds, settled against by Trion's capped options.
- **The desk** lists fully collateralized capped options on the compute index — perpetuals are built into the protocol for a later listing — so buyers and sellers of a GPU-hour can trade its price, not physical delivery.
- **The methodology** — venues, weights, trimming, the self-deviation screen and the fixing — is published in full: [benchmark statement](https://github.com/Trionmarket/trion/blob/main/docs/public/benchmark-statement.md) and [methodology factsheet](https://github.com/Trionmarket/trion/blob/main/docs/public/methodology-factsheet.md).
